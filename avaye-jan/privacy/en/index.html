Avaye Jan privacy policy

Updated 4 November 2026

Controller and contact

Avaye Jan is operated by Arman Nakhaeirad, trading as DevPars, An Sankt Ignatius 15, 45128 Essen, Germany. For privacy questions or requests, email nakhaie@hotmail.com. The producer credit in the app is Arman Nakhai Rad.

Data kept on your device

There is no separate Avaye Jan account. Instrument recordings, layered projects, tuning, favorites, language and lesson progress are stored on your device. The app does not automatically upload this musical content to us.

Microphone and melody replay

The optional listening feature requests microphone permission when used. A short single melody is analyzed in device memory to estimate notes. Raw microphone audio is not saved as a microphone recording or transmitted off the device. You can deny or revoke permission and still play the touch instruments.

Sharing performances

When sharing a WAV, you select the recipient or app in the Android share chooser. That selected file is handed to the destination you choose, whose own privacy practices apply. Deleting a local take does not delete previously shared copies.

Purchases and verification, once activated

Purchases and restore are being prepared for Google Play only. We do not receive full card or bank details. Verification sends purchase evidence, store, app/product identifiers, a random installation identifier and a one-time request value to our verification service. Raw evidence, installation identifier and request value are processed transiently. The verification ledger retains only a cryptographic purchase digest, store/product information and verification timestamps, not raw card data, email or audio. This service is not deployed yet; its host and processing details will be added here before activation.

Google advertising, once activated

Ad-enabled free editions may show a Google AdMob banner in the gallery. Google and configured advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Required privacy messages appear before eligible ad requests. Where required, About provides an entry point to change advertising privacy choices. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop all processing for permitted limited ads. Paid access removes banners.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it in the app, clear app data or uninstall; shared copies remain with their recipients. Once purchases are activated, the verification ledger is retained to maintain lifetime access and resolve purchase disputes, with no automatic fixed-day expiry. You may request deletion; records needed for a legal obligation or claim may be retained until that need ends. Connection identifiers for request-rate protection are processed briefly in service memory. Hosting log retention will be disclosed before the service goes live.

Requesting data or deletion

Email the contact above with the subject “Avaye Jan privacy” and describe your request. Do not initially send a password or raw purchase token. If ownership verification is needed, we will arrange an appropriate method privately. Deleting device data does not automatically refund a purchase. Records independently controlled by the store or Google are managed through those providers.

Support, security and legal bases

Support messages you choose to send arrive in our Outlook/Hotmail mailbox and are retained as needed to answer and follow up, or as legally required. Purchase and contractual support processing is based on performance of a contract; abuse prevention relies on legitimate interests. Consent is obtained separately where required. App-service connections use HTTPS, and publisher-side access is restricted to people and services needed for operation and security.

This website and international processing

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from this website; these pages have no tracking scripts, cookies or upload forms. GitHub processes technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. GitHub, Microsoft and Google may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States; their linked privacy notices explain their processing and safeguards. The purchase-service hosting provider has not yet been selected.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent through available controls and complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the authority for North Rhine-Westphalia. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us using the address above.

Changes
