Help and bug reports

Please include your phone model, app version, app language and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot of the affected screen can help. Do not send passwords, full card details or a confidential purchase token in your first message.

Recordings and files

Instrument recordings and layered projects are stored on your device. Before clearing app data or uninstalling, share recordings you want to keep to a destination you choose. Deleting a local file does not delete copies already shared.

Microphone and melody listening

Microphone access is only needed for the optional melody-listening feature. Touch instruments work without it. Use a short single melody in a quiet place; this feature does not transcribe a full song or several simultaneous instruments.

Lifetime purchase and restore

The intended one-time price in EUR storefronts is €4.99; this does not mean purchases are active. Once configured, the store shows the final local price before payment. Restore requires the Google Play account used for the purchase.

About Avaye Jan

Produced by Arman Nakhai Rad. The app supports Persian, English and German. Audio and score sources and licenses are listed in About. Visuals draw on Persian art; the app does not replace a complete instrument or radif course.
