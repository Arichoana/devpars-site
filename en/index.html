Frequently asked questions

Are projects limited to one programming language?
No. The offer starts with your project goal. We assess suitable technology before committing; I do not promise unchecked support for every platform. My long-standing specialty is C#/.NET and business applications.
What do the EUR99 and EUR199 packages include?
A landing page with up to four sections, or a simple website with up to three pages, a shared layout, one language and contact links. You supply approved copy and images. One revision round is included; extra features need a separate quote first.
Can you also build a shop or a larger application?
We can first assess those projects and split them into practical stages. They are outside the starter packages. Scope, technical fit, cost and available capacity must be confirmed before any commitment.
Do you work entirely remotely?
Yes. We work online, with written updates and video calls by arrangement. I currently do not offer on-site appointments.
Do you guarantee Google rankings or legal compliance?
I improve the agreed technical foundations. I cannot guarantee a Google position or legal compliance. You provide legal texts or have them reviewed by a qualified professional.

What would you like to build or improve?

Describe your project, the essential features and approximate timing. Include a link if you already have a website. I will review the scope and suggest a suitable starting point.
